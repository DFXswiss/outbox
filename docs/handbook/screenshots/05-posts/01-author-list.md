# Author post list

The page shows the Outbox brand and New post and Posts navigation, with Posts as the heading. The composer sees their own post cards and no card from another author. Each card shows a status chip, scheduled date and time, post text, a plain-language status message, and an Open link. Long post text is shortened at a word boundary and ends with “…” (a single word longer than the limit is cut at the character limit). The visible examples include submitted with “Waiting for approval.”, approved and pending with “Approved. Goes out at the scheduled time.”, sent with “Published on X.” and a “View on X” link, failed with “Sending failed. An approver can retry.”, and rejected with “Rejected by an approver.” The rejected post includes the approver’s comment. The image post also shows “Image attached.”
