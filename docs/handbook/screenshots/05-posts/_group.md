# Posts

The navigation links to New post and Posts. Composers see only posts from their account, with status chips, scheduled times, post text, optional “Image attached” labels, a plain-language status message, any decision comment, and an Open link. Text and comments longer than 120 characters are shortened at a word boundary and end with “…”; a single word longer than the limit is cut at the character limit. Approvers see posts from every author. An empty composer list offers “Write a post”; an empty approver list does not.
