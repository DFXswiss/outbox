# Approver post list

The page shows the Outbox brand and New post and Posts navigation, with Posts as the heading. The approver list includes cards from the composer and a second author. Each card shows a status chip, scheduled date and time, post text, a plain-language status message, and an Open link. The rejected post includes its approver comment. Long post text is shortened at a word boundary and ends with “…”; a single word longer than the limit is cut at the character limit.
